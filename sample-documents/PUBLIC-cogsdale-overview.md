## Demo content notice

This summary was prepared from Cogsdale's public website for the CSR Assist
demonstration. It is not official product documentation, a contractual
commitment, or implementation guidance. Verify product capabilities with
Cogsdale before making a purchasing or operational decision.

## Company and platform

Cogsdale describes itself as a provider of customer information system (CIS)
and enterprise resource planning (ERP) software for utilities and local
governments. Its public website identifies Cogsdale as a Microsoft Solutions
Partner and references Microsoft Azure and Dynamics 365 Business Central.

## Utility billing

Cogsdale Customer Service Management (CSM) supports convergent billing across
services such as electric, telecommunications, water, wastewater, stormwater,
sewer, gas, refuse, internet, telephone, and cable.

The public utility-billing overview lists consolidated customer and premise
information, activity history, payment arrangements, complex billing,
reporting, document management, and data security and compliance among the CSM
capabilities.

The CSM product page states that the system supports flexible rates, unlimited
routes, multiple billing plans, meter data from leading providers, complex
rate calculations, and bills covering multiple services.

## Customer engagement and operations

Cogsdale presents a Customer Engagement Portal as a self-service option
intended to increase customer touchpoints and reduce call volume. Its public
product navigation also lists mobile service-order management, outage
management, and payment kiosks as utility-billing-related offerings.
