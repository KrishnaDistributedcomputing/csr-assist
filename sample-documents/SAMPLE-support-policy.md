## Sample content only

This file is demonstration content. It is not an actual company policy.

## Returns

Sample purchases may be returned within 30 calendar days when the customer has
proof of purchase. Items must be unused and include their original packaging.

## Damaged deliveries

For this sample workflow, representatives should ask the customer for the order
number and a photograph of the damaged package. Representatives should not
promise a refund until the claim has been reviewed.

## Escalation

Sample cases involving safety concerns should be escalated to the duty manager
before customer-ready guidance is provided.
