## Demo content notice

This synthetic scenario exists only to demonstrate CSR Assist search,
grounding, and citation behavior. It does not describe a real company,
commercial product, contractual commitment, or implementation.

## Service platform

The fictional service platform supports customer-service operations for
electric, water, wastewater, gas, refuse, internet, and other utility
services.

## Utility billing

The platform supports consolidated customer and premise information, account
activity, payment arrangements, billing plans, rate calculations, reporting,
document management, and bills that cover multiple services.

Representatives can use the platform to review account context, explain
approved billing policies, and prepare responses for customer review. Final
decisions and customer communications remain the responsibility of trained
representatives.

## Customer engagement and operations

The synthetic scenario includes a customer portal, service-order management,
outage communications, and payment support. These examples provide realistic
topics for testing retrieval and citations without representing an actual
vendor.
